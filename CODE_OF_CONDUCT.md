# Community conduct

Help make Sentinel a respectful and useful place to collaborate.

- Be considerate of people with different backgrounds, experience and viewpoints.
- Discuss technical decisions with specific evidence and constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing another person's information or footage.
- Avoid harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks and unwanted contact.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Include relevant links and context without distributing private information publicly.
